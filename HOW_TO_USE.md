# Restaurant Launch Dashboard — How to Use

---

## What is this app?

A dashboard that helps restaurant owners manage everything they need to open a new restaurant — tasks, budget, menu, staff, permits, and marketing — all in one place.

---

## How to Sign Up

1. Open the app in your browser.
2. Click **"Don't have an account? Sign up"**.
3. Enter your **name**, **email**, and **password**.
4. Click **Create Account**.
5. You're in — your dashboard opens automatically.

---

## How to Sign In

1. Open the app.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.

To sign out, click your name in the top bar and select **Sign Out**.

---

## The 6 Main Sections

### Dashboard
Your home base. See your launch progress, budget summary, and recent activity at a glance.

Also includes an **AI Assistant** — ask it anything about your launch like "What tasks are overdue?" or "How is my budget doing?"

---

### Planning
- **Calendar** — See all your due dates, interviews, training, and marketing posts in one calendar view.
- **Timeline** — Track your major launch milestones by phase (Pre-Launch → Construction → Staffing → Launch).
- **Task Board** — Add and manage every to-do item. Set priority, due date, and who's responsible. Break tasks into subtasks.
- **Notes** — Write and organize notes by topic. Attach files.

---

### Kitchen
- **Menu Planner** — Build your full menu with ingredient costs. The app calculates your food cost % automatically.
- **Shopping List** — Auto-generated from your menu. Groups ingredients by vendor for easy ordering.
- **Cost Calculator** — See projected profit based on your menu pricing and costs.

---

### Operations
- **Inventory** — Track stock levels and set par levels. Get alerts when items run low.
- **Vendors** — Store all your supplier contact info and delivery schedules.
- **Permits** — Track every permit and license with expiry dates. Get alerts before anything expires.
- **Daily Checklists** — Build AM and PM shift checklists for your opening team.

---

### Financials
- **Budget** — Enter your startup costs and operating costs. Track budgeted vs. actual spending to catch overruns early.
- **Invoices** — Log every vendor invoice, mark it Paid/Pending/Overdue, and attach the file.

---

### Team
- **Hiring** — List every position you need to fill with urgency, pay rate, and status.
- **Candidates** — Move applicants through stages: Applied → Interviewed → Trial Shift → Hired. Score trial shifts.
- **Onboarding** — Build a checklist of steps every new hire needs to complete.
- **Training** — Create training modules with steps and videos for your FOH and BOH teams.
- **Marketing** — Plan and schedule your pre-opening social media posts across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, etc.
- **Digital Assets** — Store logos, brand files, and platform passwords securely (PIN-protected).

---

## PIN Lock

Some sections (hiring pay rates, digital assets, marketing credentials) are locked behind a **4-digit PIN**.

Default PIN: **1379**

Change it in Settings the first time you log in.

---

## A Few Quick Tips

- **Start with the Task Board.** Add every task you can think of on Day 1. It connects to the Calendar and Timeline automatically.
- **Update your Budget weekly.** Five minutes every Monday keeps you from being surprised by overruns.
- **Use the AI.** Before any owner meeting, ask it "What's overdue?" — it reads your real data.
- **Score every trial shift.** The notes you take now will help you decide who to hire when you're running on no sleep.
- **Lock the PIN** before sharing your screen with contractors or vendors.
