## The Big Picture

## What's Inside & How It Helps You

### DASHBOARD — Your Daily Check-In
**Where to go first every time you open the app.**

- **Launch Progress Ring** — One number that shows how ready you are (0–100%). Watch it climb as tasks get done.
- **Priority Focus** — The app automatically surfaces the 4 most important incomplete tasks. No more wondering "what should I do today?"
- **Overdue Alerts** — Red banners warn you if anything critical is falling behind before it becomes a crisis.
- **Recent Activity** — See what your partner last updated so you're never out of the loop.

---

### PLANNING — Keep the Launch on Schedule

#### Calendar
Every task, marketing post, training session, and interview shows up on one calendar. You can see the whole month at a glance and add events directly from here.

#### Timeline
Big milestones organized by phase — Construction → Staffing → Training → Launch. Check things off as they're completed. Great for a quick "where are we?" conversation.

#### Tasks
The heart of the planning section. Tasks are organized by department:
- **Lease & TI** (tenant improvements, build-out)
- **Menu & Bar**
- **Permits**
- **Staffing**
- **Financials**
- **Operations**

Each task has a status (Not Started / In Progress / Complete / Overdue), priority level, due date, and a sub-checklist for the details. Filter by department or status to find exactly what you need fast.

#### Notes & Ideas
A scratch pad for both of you. Tag notes by topic (Vendor, Menu, Operations) and search them later. You can also save notes directly to Google Drive.

---

### KITCHEN — Menu & Food Costs

#### Menu & Bar Planner
Build out the full menu with sections for Small Plates, Shared Plates, Catering, Drinks, Wine, and Specials. For each item you can track:
- Price and food cost %
- Ingredients and their individual costs
- Photos
- Whether it's a hero/featured dish

Items with food cost over 30% are automatically flagged — so you catch margin problems before the menu is printed.

#### Shopping List
Automatically pulls every ingredient from every menu item and consolidates them into one master list. Filter by Kitchen, Bar, or Front of House. Print it and hand it to whoever is doing the ordering.

---

### OPERATIONS — The Behind-the-Scenes Stuff

#### Inventory
Track every item you need — what's arrived, what's been ordered, what's still needed. Set par levels so you always know when to reorder.

#### Vendors
Keep all your vendor contacts in one place — name, phone, email, delivery days, and category. No more searching through texts to find your seafood rep's number.

#### Permits & Licenses
Track your Health Permit, Liquor License, Fire Certificate, and Business License — including expiry dates. You'll get alerts before anything expires.

#### Utility Accounts
Log your electric, gas, water, and internet accounts with account numbers and monthly costs. No more digging through emails to find the login for the internet provider.

#### Daily Checklists
AM and PM checklists for FOH opening and Kitchen closing. Build the checklist once — your team uses it every day.

---

### FINANCIALS — Know Your Numbers (PIN Protected)

This section requires a PIN to open — keeps sensitive data secure.

#### Financials Tracker
- **Startup Costs** — Budget vs. actual for every category (build-out, equipment, inventory, marketing, etc.). Color-coded bars show you instantly if you're over or under budget.
- **Operating Costs** — Monthly payroll, food cost, rent, utilities, insurance, and more.
- **KPIs** — Total startup budget, monthly break-even number, and projected revenue.

#### Invoices
Log every invoice as it comes in, link it to a vendor, and mark it paid or pending. The totals flow automatically into your financial tracker.

---

### TEAM — Hiring & Training

#### Talent & Hiring (PIN Protected)
Track every open position — GM, Executive Chef, Servers, Bartenders, and any custom roles. For each candidate you can record:
- What stage they're in (Interviewed → Trial Shift → Offered → Hired)
- Trial shift scores (technique, speed, vibe)
- Your notes and partner notes separately

The hiring progress bar shows how close you are to a full team at a glance.

#### Training Portal
Create training modules for FOH and BOH with video links and step-by-step checklists. Track who has completed what before opening day.

#### Marketing Calendar
Plan and track every social media post, website update, and promotional content. See what's posted, scheduled, or still a draft — organized by platform (Instagram, Facebook, etc.).

## What This App Can Do For You

Opening a restaurant means managing a hundred moving pieces at the same time — permits, build-out, hiring, menu costs, vendors, training, marketing — and most of it is happening all at once. This app is built to hold all of that in one place so nothing slips through the cracks and both of you are always looking at the same picture.

The **Dashboard** is your morning check-in. It shows you a live progress ring for the entire launch, automatically flags the 4 most urgent tasks you should focus on today, and fires a red alert if anything critical is overdue. You can also see everything your partner updated since you last logged in — no more "did you handle that?" texts.

The **Planning section** is where the work gets organized. Tasks are sorted by department — Lease, Menu, Permits, Staffing, Financials, Operations — and each one has a status, a priority level, a due date, and a sub-checklist so nothing gets half-done. A shared **Calendar** puts every task, interview, training session, and marketing post on one timeline so you can see how the weeks ahead actually look. The **Timeline** tracks your big milestones phase by phase — Construction, Staffing, Training, Launch — so you always know where you are in the journey. And **Notes & Ideas** gives both of you a shared notepad to capture vendor conversations, menu ideas, and anything else that needs to be written down before it's forgotten.

The **Kitchen section** takes the guesswork out of your menu. You can build your full menu with pricing, ingredients, food cost percentages, and photos — and anything over 30% food cost gets automatically flagged so you fix your margins before the menu goes to print. A **Shopping List** is auto-generated from your menu items and consolidates every ingredient with total quantities and costs, ready to print and hand off.

The **Operations section** keeps the behind-the-scenes running smoothly. **Inventory** tracks everything you need with vendor info, arrival status, and par levels. **Vendors** stores every contact — name, phone, email, delivery days — so you stop hunting through texts for a phone number. **Permits & Licenses** tracks your Health Permit, Liquor License, Fire Certificate, and Business License with expiry date alerts so nothing lapses. **Utility Accounts** stores your electric, gas, water, and internet account numbers and monthly costs in one secure place. And **Daily Checklists** lets you build AM and PM shift checklists for your team that they use every single day.

The **Financials section** (PIN protected) gives you a real-time view of your money. You can track every startup expense against your budget with color-coded bars that instantly show over or under. Monthly operating costs — payroll, food cost, rent, utilities, insurance — are tracked separately so you know your break-even. Every invoice gets logged, linked to a vendor, and feeds directly into the totals. You can export everything to a spreadsheet anytime.

The **Team section** manages hiring and training from start to finish. Every open position is listed with how many are filled. Every candidate has a profile — what stage they're in, trial shift scores, your notes and partner notes. A progress bar shows how close you are to a full team. Once hired, **Training** lets you build step-by-step modules with video links and track who has completed what before opening day. The **Marketing Calendar** keeps your social posts organized by platform and status — Draft, Scheduled, or Posted — so your launch content doesn't get chaotic.

The **AI Assistant** lives at the top of every page. Ask it anything about your data — "what tasks are overdue?", "what's our current food cost?", "which permits are still pending?" — and it answers using your actual information, not generic advice.

And no matter where you are in the app, the **+ button** in the bottom right corner lets you instantly add a task, write a note, or log an invoice without breaking your flow. Both of you see every update in real time — no syncing, no emailing files back and forth, no version confusion.

## Quick Tips for Staying Organized

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Just walked in, need to know what's urgent | Check the **Dashboard** — Priority Focus + any red alerts |
| Want to see the big picture on timing | Open **Timeline** and check milestones |
| Need to find a vendor's phone number | Go to **Vendors** |
| Reviewing food costs before finalizing the menu | Open **Menu Planner**, look for items flagged red |
| Checking if you're on budget | Open **Financials → Startup Costs** |
| Hiring status | Open **Talent & Hiring** |
| Something just came up that needs tracking | Hit the **+ button** (bottom right corner) to add a task or note instantly |
